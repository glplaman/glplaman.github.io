DataFrame

2026-06-24
Overview
是一种表格数据结构
多维数据：行 row 和列 column 组成
带标签
构造数据，创建 DataFrame
data={
  'name':['alice','mike','bob','jonh','marry'],
  'age':[18,20,15,17,19]
}

df=pd.DataFrame(data)
结果：每一行都有一个索引
    name  age
0  alice   18
1   mike   20
2    bob   15
3   jonh   17
4  marry   19
为索引指定标签
df=pd.DataFrame(data,index=['classmate 1','classmate 2','classmate 3','classmate 4','classmate 5'])
结果
              name  age
classmate 1  alice   18
classmate 2   mike   20
classmate 3    bob   15
classmate 4   jonh   17
classmate 5  marry   19
根据行标签访问数据
df.loc['classmate 1']
结果
name    alice
age        18
Name: classmate 1, dtype: object
根据行索引访问；结果同上
df.iloc[0]
增加数据列 column - 性别 gender
个数要一一对应
df['gender']=['male','male','female','male','female']
增加数据行 row
构造新 dataFrame，连接 concat 到原数据
索引可以稍后指定；因为其它数据指定过索引，所以新行的索引是0
r=pd.DataFrame([{'name':'ben','age':19,'gender':'male'}])
df=pd.concat([df,r])
结果
              name  age  gender
classmate 1  alice   18    male
classmate 2   mike   20    male
classmate 3    bob   15  female
classmate 4   jonh   17    male
classmate 5  marry   19  female
0              ben   19    male
增加多行并指定标签
rs=pd.DataFrame([{'name':'rose','age':18,'gender':'female'},{'name':'jack','age':21,'gender':'male'}],index=['classmate 6','classmate 7'])
df=pd.concat([df,rs])
结果
              name  age  gender
classmate 1  alice   18    male
classmate 2   mike   20    male
classmate 3    bob   15  female
classmate 4   jonh   17    male
classmate 5  marry   19  female
0              ben   19    male
classmate 6   rose   18  female
classmate 7   jack   21    male
